Loadout

The export already includes everything you had equipped, so every adjustment here starts switched off and the numbers match the game exactly. Nothing on this page needs touching to get correct rates.

It is for "what if" questions. Each row is one thing that changes a stat: a drone, a bomb, an item. At export is whether the game had it when you copied the export. In calc is whether you want the maths to assume it. Leave them equal and the row does nothing. Tick In calc on something you did not have, and the rates change as if you had it.

The export does not record which bomb you throw, so pick it above. Ores without a card of their own ignore the card values and stay at 1x; the bar card also scales the gold value of a bar.

Which bomb

Bomb of Plenty multiplies the ore you dig, so the extra ore gets crafted. The Transmuter drops bars directly and can also stamp a BoP stack on the rock. Which one wins comes down to how many ore a bar costs, so the crossover follows the ore tier rather than the floor number.

Credits

The maths, the floor data and the whole structure of this came from the IOM Calcs spreadsheet by Taikuh. Without that sheet there is nothing here to port. If a number looks wrong, it is far more likely to be my transposition than their work.

The bomb crossover works the way tabatkins' Transmuter calculator does it, which models the Transmuter reapplying BoP, rather than the simpler add-up in the sheet. That tool is also the reason this page reports a break-even instead of a winner per floor.

The sheet itself credits the IOM Wiki for images, numbers and mechanics, the Total Resources Calculator for the quick-fill approach, Best Farming Floor as the backbone of the floor work, and Let's Go Fishing for the interface style.
